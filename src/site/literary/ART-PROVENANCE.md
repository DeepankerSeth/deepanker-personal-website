# Artwork provenance

The four current illustrations are original AI-assisted interpretations composed for the published essays and poem. They depict imagined paper-and-ink constructions; they are not scans of physical work. No borrowed artwork, traced external contour or stock image is embedded. The public colophon discloses AI assistance.

The approved lossless masters, selected generation/edit identifiers, hashes and consolidated prompt specifications are preserved in [artwork/masters](../../../artwork/masters/README.md), outside the served directory. Historical origin paths in that evidence are not runtime dependencies. Optimised display assets live under `public/literary/art/`; fonts retain their licences alongside their files.

[The artwork workflow](../../../docs/ARTWORK.md) is the current instruction for new pieces, rights, encoding, registry integration and verification. The selected passages remain exact author text; the visual interpretation and its explanatory note are design work, not claims made by the author. Curated art is withheld if the relevant quotations no longer match the published piece or a different Index passage is selected.
