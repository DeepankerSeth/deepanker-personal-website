# Design learnings

This records the commission, not permanent rules about Deepanker's taste. **Explicit feedback** comes from his requests; **design interpretation** describes our reading of the writing. Interpretations do not establish his identity. These lessons should guide evolution, not freeze it.

## The ambition and the evidence

Deepanker wanted a public expression of his writing, thinking and taste: an immediate invitation, discoveries that reward attention, and comfortable sustained reading. He explicitly valued complete titles, immediate choice, changing colour atmospheres, generous composition, meaningful artwork and responsive motion. Mobile deserves at least equal creative attention.

He repeatedly rejected generic polish, excessive restraint and unintuitive exploration. “An index in motion” was a foundation to develop. Later redesigns weakened its clarity; simply restoring it unchanged also failed the brief. Approval of a quality was evidence about taste, not approval of a whole composition or permission to stop improving it.

## Three directions, one relationship

| Direction | Explicitly valued | Limitation and lesson |
|---|---|---|
| **The living index** | Immediate choice, complete titles and a collection brought to life by attention. | Its abstract graphics lacked a consequential relationship to the words. Keep directness; give attention somewhere meaningful to go. |
| **The illuminated page** | Intimacy between an exact passage and an artwork interpreting it, especially the layers, shared masks and reading of *Meditations*. | Recognisably AI-generated finish weakened conviction. Emotional connection did not excuse uniform texture, familiar symbolism or excessive polish. |
| **Four folded folios** | Precise construction, dimensionality, material intelligence and responsive movement. | Its software-product character needed reinterpretation. Carry its craft into a literary world instead of importing its whole visual system. |

Historical views: [Living index](references/living-index.jpg), [Illuminated page](references/illuminated-page.jpg), [Folded folios](references/folded-folios.jpg). These are comparison evidence, not current palettes or runnable alternatives.

The synthesis gives each quality a role: the index makes choice legible; a passage supplies the emotional and intellectual centre; material artwork offers another reading; atmosphere and light respond to attention. The essay then settles into stillness. It works through these relationships, not by assembling attractive components from three studies.

## Read closely enough to resist the obvious image

Our interpretation notices observation questioning itself, roles exchanging places, the observer's involvement in a pattern, and belonging without confinement. These readings come from lines, not personality labels.

- In *Abstractions*, an early printed eye implied mystical surveillance. A profile formed by the veil's cut edge keeps the observer inside the layers. No final window promises escape from them all.
- In *The Hidden War*, separate polished masks made an attractive emblem. One imperfect shared sheet better holds opposing roles made of the same material. Neither face signifies moral innocence.
- In *Meditations*, an opening and unresolved ends leave room for repeated observation. A perfectly solved knot would overstate the essay.
- In *I am*, moth, lily and snow come from the poem; their arrangement is imagined. It does not establish the writer's religious identity.

New writing may deserve a different medium, degree of detail or kind of silence. Do not repeat these symbols indefinitely.

## Failures worth remembering

**Expression needs a purpose.** Larger type, more movement and fashionable colours weakened earlier versions when they obscured choosing and reading. “A field of passages” was rejected as AI-looking in this commission; that is contextual evidence, not a universal ban on poetic layouts.

**Simplicity must retain substance.** The flat synthesis lost the depth and intimacy the author valued. Remove competing controls and redundant explanation; do not flatten ambitious artwork into generic minimalism. Conversely, uniform distress, copper rims and flawless symmetry made images feel synthetic. Quiet material and selective detail were stronger.

**Motion must survive use.** A travelling-art transition crossed incoming titles and prose. Direct navigation preserved a clear reading entrance while keeping index atmosphere and local light. Stale dialog-close events also disrupted rapid reopening: expressive continuity depends on correct state, focus and scroll restoration. The hybrid moves light, not painted geometry.

**Mobile needs its own composition.** The retained phone refinement puts the exact passage and nearby actions before the artwork, inside the selected title's encounter. Complete titles remain direct links; exploration is optional. Some poster-like scale is sacrificed for a more immediate invitation. Touch responds to intentional choice and then settles, rather than changing atmosphere during incidental scrolling.

## Keep evolving with judgment

Start with the whole piece and a concrete question: does this image make a line newly perceptible, or this gesture clarify a relationship? Compare consequential changes with the saved version at equivalent sizes and states, including long titles and poetry. Inspect motion, returns and failures. Tests prove bounded functionality, not beauty; screenshots alone do not establish scrolling or comfort.

Cross traditions—books, printmaking, exhibitions, illustration and interactive art—when they produce one coherent personal experience. Pursue difficult ideas through revision, leave room for uncertainty and the reader's interpretation.
