# Preserved artwork sources

The four selected PNG masters, generation/edit identifiers, prompt specifications and source hashes are retained here, outside the served `public/` tree. These are transparent raster masters, not physical sculptures, scans or editable vector/layer stacks. The composition and light are editable in `src/site/literary/MaterialArt.astro`, `artworks.ts` and `material-motion.js`.

[Prompt specifications](imagegen/PROMPTS.md) and [provenance](imagegen/provenance.json) preserve the original generation record. Their old output paths identify origin, not live dependencies. The current process, worked example and rights requirements are in [Artwork](../../docs/ARTWORK.md). Superseded prototype integration/review notes remain in the private pre-maintenance recovery bundle.

The commission's research considered Yulia Brodskaya's process, Su Blackwell's AccessArt introduction and Martin Lewis's *Corner Shadows*. These informed material principles; no work from those artists was embedded or traced. The earlier research record, including access limitations, remains in the recovery bundle's original ART-NOTES.md. The artwork is explicitly AI-assisted, not an independently commissioned physical artwork.

Keep future artist-owned editable files, source licences and revision history alongside the approved master. A prompt cannot reproduce a master. Only optimised display assets belong in `public/`.
